# AppWorldAI Channel Handoff

Updated: 2026-06-25

Channel: AppWorldAI / app-world-ai

## Mission

AppWorldAI is an AI builder channel: news, practical experiments, app/game/site builds, and creator-business lessons. The channel should feel useful to people who want to understand what new AI tools can actually do, not just hear hype.

Primary promise:

- Find new AI tools and releases fast.
- Turn them into understandable demos.
- Show practical builds with OBS screen capture.
- Promote the founder's apps naturally as relevant sponsors.

## Publishing Cadence

Baseline cadence:

- 2 long-form videos per week.
- 2-4 Shorts per week when a topic is too small for a full video or is breaking news.
- Review the AI news queue every 2 days.

Best posting rhythm:

- Monday or Tuesday: hands-on build, tutorial, or tool test.
- Thursday or Friday: AI news roundup, comparison, or business/use-case breakdown.
- Shorts: same day as news drops, or clipped from the long-form recording.

## Content Pillars

1. AI News That Matters
   - New models, agents, app builders, coding agents, video generators, voice tools, search agents, and business automation.
   - Angle: "What changed, who should care, and what can I build with it?"

2. Build With Me
   - Use AI to build games, apps, websites, landing pages, automations, and app features.
   - Angle: "I gave an AI agent this task. Here's what worked, what broke, and what I shipped."

3. AI Tool Battles
   - Compare tools on the same real task.
   - Examples: Codex vs Claude Code, Google AI Studio vs OpenAI, Veo vs Runway, app builders, website builders.

4. Founder App Promotion
   - Sponsor reads for owned products.
   - The sponsor should match the topic so it feels connected, not random.

5. AI Business Playbook
   - Show how a solo founder can use AI to launch and market products.
   - Cover workflow, cost, mistakes, and speed.

## Episode Decision Formula

Use this every 2 days when reviewing AI topics.

Make a Short when:

- The news is simple and can be explained in under 60 seconds.
- There is no hands-on demo yet.
- The topic is mostly "new feature / release / pricing / availability."
- The point is one sharp takeaway.

Make a long-form video when:

- A tool can be tested live.
- There is a real build or comparison.
- The topic affects builders, founders, creators, or app owners.
- At least 3 sections can be shown on screen.
- There is a natural sponsor tie-in.

Bundle several items into one long-form when:

- Each item is interesting but too small alone.
- They share a theme, such as "AI agents this week" or "new AI tools for building apps."

## Long-Form Script Formula

Target length: 8-14 minutes.

1. Cold open, 0:00-0:20
   - Start with the result or the big claim.
   - Example: "AI agents just crossed a line. Today I'm going to use one to plan, code, and ship part of an app workflow."

2. Context, 0:20-1:30
   - What happened?
   - Why does it matter?
   - Who should care?

3. Sponsor, 1:30-2:00
   - One relevant owned app.

4. Demo or breakdown, 2:00-9:00
   - OBS window capture.
   - Show the tool, prompt, output, errors, fixes, and final result.

5. Founder takeaway, 9:00-11:00
   - Could this save time?
   - Is it production-ready?
   - What would you use it for?

6. Final verdict, 11:00-12:30
   - 3 bullet verdict: good, bad, who it is for.

7. CTA, final 20 seconds
   - "Subscribe to AppWorldAI. I test AI tools by building real apps with them."

## Shorts Formula

Target length: 30-55 seconds.

1. Hook:
   - "This new AI update matters if you build apps."
   - "AI agents are getting closer to doing real work."
   - "This is not just another chatbot update."

2. One fact:
   - Cite the source on screen or in the description.

3. One practical use:
   - "For builders, this means..."

4. One question:
   - "Would you trust this with your app?"

5. CTA:
   - "Follow AppWorldAI for AI tools tested on real builds."

## Weekly Research Workflow

Every Monday and Thursday:

1. Search official sources first:
   - OpenAI news
   - Anthropic news
   - Google AI blog
   - Apple Newsroom and developer news
   - Microsoft, GitHub, Meta, xAI, Runway, Perplexity, Hugging Face, Canva, Adobe

2. Then check creator/news sources:
   - Matt Wolfe
   - Riley Brown / similar AI-builder creators
   - The Rundown AI
   - Ben's Bites
   - Artificial Intelligence News
   - Product Hunt AI
   - YouTube search for "AI tools this week"

3. Score each topic:
   - Freshness: 1-5
   - Builder usefulness: 1-5
   - Demo potential: 1-5
   - Sponsor fit: 1-5
   - Total score: 16+ means long-form candidate, 10-15 means Short or roundup item.

4. Before writing:
   - Confirm release date.
   - Confirm availability and pricing if mentioned.
   - Avoid claiming a feature is live if it is only announced or rolling out.
   - Save source links in the script.
