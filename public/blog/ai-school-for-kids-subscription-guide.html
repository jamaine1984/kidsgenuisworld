Before subscribing to Kid Genius World, parents should review the AI school limits, privacy, billing, support, and learning expectations.

Before paying for any AI school-style app, parents should understand what the product does, what it does not do, and how billing and support work.

How Kid Genius World fits

Kid Genius World should be clear that subscription access is for app learning features, not accredited school enrollment or official education credentials.

What parents can use it for

  • Read privacy, terms, and parent support pages.
  • Confirm the app is not accredited and does not issue credits.
