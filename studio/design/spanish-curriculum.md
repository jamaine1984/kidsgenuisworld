# Spanish learning studio

The illustrated Little Studio/Maker Lab and Theo are preserved. Language is a persistent public preference (`kgw:language`), accessible in the header and through `?lang=es`. It changes the active reading curriculum, math copy, parent controls, terms/privacy, narration, and the product tour.

## Content

Each grade has 200 math activities across five sections, and 240 native Spanish reading activities across six sections (40 each). Math activities and exposure identities are shared between languages. Spanish reading has its own stable `es-r…` IDs, reading history and book shelf; it does not teach translated English silent-e or short/long-vowel rules.

| Grade | Reading progression |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Vowels in words; common consonant-vowel syllables; c/qu; r; ch/ñ; longer syllable sequences; simple sentences, agreement and question marks; everyday vocabulary; short connected texts. |
| 2 | Silent h; qu and gu; ch/ll/ñ and r/rr; consonant clusters; diphthongs; longer words and stress; sentence connections; context, synonyms and opposites; ordered events and evidence. |
| 3 | Diphthongs and stressed í/ú hiatus; agudas, llanas and esdrújulas; written accents; gu/gü; prefixes and suffixes; explanations and reasons; main ideas, source clues, fluent paragraphs. |

The 30 word packs each contain eight curated spellings with explicit syllable boundaries and stress positions. Onset matching uses Latin American Spanish conventions: b/v are not presented as different phonemes, and ll/y use a yeísta pronunciation. Accent marks, ñ and ü remain significant in writing. Spoken demonstrations use real complete words and syllables, not synthesized claims of isolated phoneme accuracy.

The bank contains 60 original passages (30 stories and 30 informational texts), 24 Spanish little books and 60 original-writing prompts. Fixed text questions receive practice evidence; finite banks exhaust rather than pad lessons with repeated questions. Fluent reading and optional microphone self-checks do not establish independent mastery.

## Voice and motion

Browser speech selects a Spanish voice (es-MX preferred, then another Spanish voice). It does not select an English voice for Spanish instruction. Written directions, slow/replay/stop controls and an unavailable-voice fallback remain accessible. Microphone recognition is optional and on-device only when an existing language pack is available; no recordings or transcripts enter backups.

The Remotion tour demonstrates native Spanish syllable blending (`ma + pa → mapa`), the same accurate `8 + 5` make-ten model, and current family features. HyperFrames uses the same seekable title animation with localized copy. Browser speech is paced separately so narration can finish before advancing.

## References and limits

Curriculum sequencing uses explicit decoding, connected reading, vocabulary and writing practice. Spanish spelling/stress references and primary education guidance consulted:

- [Spanish primary language area](https://educagob.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/curriculo/curriculo-lomloe/menu-curriculos-basicos/ed-primaria/areas/lengua-castellana.html)
- [RAE vowel sequences and accent marks](https://www.rae.es/ortografía-básica/uso-de-la-tilde/las-reglas-de-acentuación-gráfica/la-acentuación-gráfica-de-las-palabras-con-secuencias-vocálicas)
- [RAE student orthography](https://www.rae.es/diccionario-estudiante/docs/ortografia.pdf)
- [IES reading support](https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/resource/other-resource/supporting-your-childs-reading-home)

The parent map uses internal `ES.…` reading goals, with an explicit explanation that they are not an official certification or English Common Core phonics alignment. Native Spanish reading was authored separately. Public English UI/math translations began as machine-assisted drafts; core directions, math units, captions and preserved brand names were corrected through code and browser review. Tests cover answer uniqueness, fixtures, syllables/stress, tiles, accents, language/history boundaries and voice selection. This is not an external educator review, a pronunciation assessment, a completed payment/cancellation test or a Google Play/device release. Legacy publisher articles and `/legacy/` remain preserved in English, labeled as such from the new app.
