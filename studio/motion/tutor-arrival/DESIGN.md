# Wonderlane Little Studio and Maker Lab

Approved on October 9 2026. Little Studio is the home visual target. Maker Lab is the lesson visual target. Use the same brown bear with the cobalt apron and yellow sun emblem everywhere. Its working name is Theo.

## Colors

- Cream canvas #fff9ef
- Deep blue text #132756
- Cobalt primary #294ee8
- Terracotta math #ca6044
- Golden reading #f0ba40
- Oak borders #e4c9a5

## Typography

Nunito at 800 and 900 for friendly headings; Nunito Sans at 400 to 800 for readable controls. Use plain upright schoolbook text for words. These match the selected visual direction and override generic motion style presets. No distorted letters or decorative text behind activities.

## Motion

Warm 250 to 450ms entrances; instructional highlights follow the explanation. Hyperframes owns the tutor arrival; Remotion owns worked examples; Three.js owns the manipulatives. Stop decorative motion while the child answers. Respect reduced motion. Never change answer positions after an attempt.

## What not to do

No purple gradients, substitute mascots, pressure timers, ranking, streak penalties, glitter over text, misleading counters, or illustrations that reveal a word being assessed. Keep one subject and one problem on screen. Native controls overlay genuine generated artwork; educational quantities render from validated data.
